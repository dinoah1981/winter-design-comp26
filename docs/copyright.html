Key Reminders for Your Film

  • You cannot use popular songs as background music — even with credit
  • Saying "I don't own the rights" or "no copyright intended" does not protect you
  • iMovie's built-in music and sound effects are free to use in your projects
  • YouTube Audio Library and Pixabay Music are safe, free alternatives
  • When in doubt, use royalty-free music from the sources above
