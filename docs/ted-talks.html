Recommended Viewing

TED Talks on Storytelling

These talks explore the art of storytelling, authentic voice, and narrative power — essential viewing for student filmmakers preparing to tell meaningful stories.

Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie 19 min

The Danger of a Single Story

A powerful reminder that reducing people or places to a single narrative robs them of dignity. Essential for documentary filmmakers learning to tell complete, authentic stories.

Watch on TED.com
Andrew Stanton 19 min

The Clues to a Great Story

The Pixar filmmaker behind Toy Story and WALL-E shares the secret ingredients of storytelling: "Make me care." Learn about character spines, narrative promises, and story structure.

Watch on TED.com
Brene Brown 20 min

The Power of Vulnerability

Understand why vulnerability and authenticity are what make stories connect with audiences. The best documentaries come from honest, courageous storytelling.

Watch on TED.com
Simon Sinek 18 min

How Great Leaders Inspire Action

Learn the "Start with Why" framework. Great films, like great leaders, begin with purpose and meaning — not just what happened, but why it matters.

Watch on TED.com
Karen Thompson Walker 12 min

What Fear Can Teach Us

Explores how our fears are really stories we tell ourselves — and how reading those stories wisely can make us better storytellers and filmmakers.

Watch on TED.com
J.J. Abrams 18 min

The Mystery Box

The filmmaker behind Lost and Star Wars explains how mystery drives storytelling. Learn how to keep your audience hooked by what they don't yet know.

Watch on TED.com
Tyler DeWitt 12 min

Hey Science Teachers — Make It Fun

A reminder that storytelling isn't about dumbing things down — it's about making your audience care. Great advice for making compelling, accessible documentaries.

Watch on TED.com
