Recommended Viewing

TED Talks on Storytelling

These talks explore the art of storytelling, authentic voice, and narrative power — essential viewing for student filmmakers preparing to tell meaningful stories.

David JP Phillips 17 min

The Magical Science of Storytelling

Why do stories captivate us? Phillips breaks down the neuroscience — how specific techniques trigger dopamine, oxytocin, and endorphins in your audience's brain. A scientific framework for emotional filmmaking.

Watch on YouTube
Andrew Stanton 19 min

The Clues to a Great Story

The Pixar filmmaker behind Toy Story and WALL-E shares the secret ingredients of storytelling: "Make me care." Learn about character spines, the 2+2 principle, and narrative promises.

Watch on YouTube
Nancy Duarte 18 min

The Secret Structure of Great Talks

Duarte reverse-engineers MLK's "I Have a Dream" speech and Steve Jobs' iPhone launch to reveal a universal narrative arc — "what is" vs. "what could be" — that you can apply to your film's storytelling.

Watch on YouTube
J.J. Abrams 18 min

The Mystery Box

The filmmaker behind Lost and Star Wars explains how mystery drives storytelling. Learn how to keep your audience hooked by what they don't yet know.

Watch on YouTube
Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie 19 min

The Danger of a Single Story

A powerful reminder that reducing people or places to a single narrative robs them of dignity. Essential for documentary filmmakers learning to tell complete, authentic, responsible stories.

Watch on YouTube
Karen Thompson Walker 12 min

What Fear Can Teach Us

Walker reframes fears as stories we tell ourselves — complete with characters, plots, and suspense — teaching you that the raw material for compelling filmmaking is already inside everyday human experience.

Watch on YouTube
Danielle Feinberg 12 min

The Magic Ingredient That Brings Pixar Movies to Life

Pixar's Director of Photography takes you behind the scenes of Finding Nemo, WALL-E, and Brave to show how lighting and visual design are storytelling tools — guiding the audience's eye and emotion.

Watch on YouTube
