Key Reminders

  • Everyone who appears in your film needs to sign a release form
  • Anyone under 18 must have a parent or guardian sign the form too
  • Get forms signed before or during filming — not after
  • Keep signed forms organized — you'll need them if your film is submitted to festivals
  • If filming on private property, get a location release from the owner
  • Even classmates and friends should sign — it's good practice and protects everyone
